# Brand Guidelines — QuickBasket

## Name

**QuickBasket** — one word, capital Q and B. Don't abbreviate in customer-facing copy (no "QB" externally).

> Note: confirm domain, app-store name availability, and trademark before heavy brand investment — flagged in `notes.md`.

## Brand Personality

- **Fast** — the name is the promise; nothing about the experience should feel slow or heavy.
- **Local** — this is a neighbourhood app, not a faceless national platform. Copy and design should feel like it knows the user's area.
- **Trustworthy** — clear pricing, honest order status, no dark patterns at checkout.
- **Friendly, not corporate** — warm and plain-spoken, especially in Hindi copy — avoid stiff, translated-sounding text.

## Voice & Tone

- Plain, short sentences. No jargon.
- English copy: warm and direct ("Get your basket delivered, fast.")
- Hindi copy: natural spoken Hindi, not literal/formal translation — write it fresh in Hindi rather than translating word-for-word from English.
- Error messages: calm and helpful, never blame the user ("Couldn't find that address — try pinning your location instead" rather than "Invalid address").

## Color (brand-level — see `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` for full UI token list)

- **Primary:** Deep bottle green (`#236837` for CTAs, `#2F8144` for accents) on warm paper (`#FBF9F3`) — fresh produce, trust, and money. This is the colour people should associate with QuickBasket at a glance.
- **Turmeric** (`#DC9A2B` lane) is reserved for offers and promotions; **khata red** (`#8E2F23` lane) is reserved for the credit ledger. Don't blend the lanes.
- Supporting neutrals and semantic colours as defined in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` ("Kirana Modern").

## Logo

- Not yet formally commissioned. Current placeholder: the Lucide `ShoppingBasket` mark in a green gradient tile (rounded 14px, subtle white inner ring) beside the wordmark **QuickBasket** set in Baloo 2 ExtraBold, with "Basket" in brand green.
- When a logo exists, document usage rules here: clear space, minimum size, do's/don'ts (no recoloring, no stretching, no placing on low-contrast backgrounds).

## Typography

- Display face: **Baloo 2** (headings, buttons, prices). Body face: **Mukta**. Both from Ek Type and both Devanagari-capable — chosen so Hindi never needs a fallback font.

## Icon Style

- Lucide icon set app-wide (see `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`) — keeps the brand visually consistent across Grocery, Food, Laundry, and Porter as they launch.

## Do

- Keep messaging benefit-led: speed, "near you," reliability.
- Use real, specific language ("Delivered in 25 minutes" beats "Fast delivery" once we have real numbers).
- Keep Hindi and English copy equally polished — Hindi shouldn't read like an afterthought translation.

## Don't

- Don't overload the homepage with promotional banners/pop-ups — the brand promise is speed and simplicity, not noise.
- Don't use urgency/pressure dark patterns ("Only 1 left!" when untrue) — trust is a stated brand pillar.
- Don't introduce a second icon set or off-brand color "just for one screen."

## As the Brand Grows

When Food, Laundry, and Porter launch, each can have a small accent identity (e.g. a secondary color per service, still Lucide icons, still the same rounded/fast visual language) — document those here once designed, rather than letting each module drift into its own look.
